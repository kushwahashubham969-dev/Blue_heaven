About Us

A small parlour that treats every visit like an occasion.

Whether you're popping in for eyebrow threading between errands or planning your bridal look months in advance, we give the same care and attention either way. Our team trained across threading, skincare, hairstyling, and bridal makeup, so every service under one roof is handled properly — not rushed.

We keep our price list transparent and posted, exactly as it stands in the parlour, so there are never any surprises when you sit down in the chair.
